About SHARE

SHARE stands for Sharing Heartland's Available Resources Equally. It is the Illinois Heartland Library System's shared library-catalog consortium, connecting member libraries across central and southern Illinois so patrons can discover and request materials.

Learn more at the official SHARE website.

Member library shortcut

Enter just the part before .illshareit.com. For example, for carp.illshareit.com, enter carp. Use 1–63 letters, numbers, or hyphens; no spaces, dots, slashes, or https://.

Catalog destination

Changing this setting affects future searches only. The extension uses the same keyword-search format as the original.

Find your library's subdomain

  1. Open the SHARE Library Locator.
  2. Enter your town or address in Enter your home address and choose a suggested location.
  3. Use the within selector to choose a search radius (6, 12, or 24 miles).
  4. Find your member library in the results. Its listing displays a shortcut address such as carp.illshareit.com.
  5. Copy only the first part (carp in this example) into the field above, then choose Save settings.

A town or city is usually enough to start the search; you do not need to enter your full home address if you prefer not to.

Only configure a shortcut shown for your member library by SHARE. This extension sends the text you select to that catalog's search URL when you choose Search SHARE.
